   379→Work Log:
   380→- Created mini-services/yoga-worker/ as an independent bun project on port
   381→  3004 with `bun --hot` for auto-restart.
   382→- The worker ticks every 10s and runs three jobs on a schedule:
   383→  - remind:   every 60s   (with 4s jitter)
   384→  - retry:    every 300s  (15s jitter)
   385→  - generate: every 300s  (30s jitter)
